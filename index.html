🏸Beginner's Guide to Badminton




📋Basic Rules


• You must serve the shuttlecock diagonally to the net.

• Hit the shuttlecock over the net.

• The first player to reach 21 points win the game.

• Only one contact with the shuttlecock to send it over.
🥅Badminton Equipment


• Beginners should use a lightweight racket.

• Use plastic or traditional feather ones for matches.

• The net stands at a standard height of 5'1 at the posts.

• Wear non-marking court shoes to prevent slipping on the floor.
